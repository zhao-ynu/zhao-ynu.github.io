Research Overview

Our research aims to build efficient, continuous, and trustworthy services for dynamic low-altitude intelligent networks through joint communication, computing, intelligence, and security design.

01

Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks

UAV-assisted communication and computing, mobile access, deployment, communication-computing coordination, and resource management.

Keywords: UAV networks · low-altitude economy · resource allocation · communication-computing integration

02

Collaborative Edge Computing

Task offloading, service caching and migration, containerized execution, and long-term online scheduling in mobile edge systems.

Keywords: MEC · task offloading · service migration · caching · online scheduling

03

Secure and Trustworthy Edge Intelligence

Federated and collaborative learning under heterogeneous and adversarial environments, including secure aggregation, poisoning/backdoor defense, covert service interaction, and AI-RAN.

Keywords: federated learning · backdoor defense · trustworthy AI · covert communication · AI-RAN
