Research Interests

My research centers on collaborative computing and security management for low-altitude intelligent networks, with an emphasis on the joint design of communication, computing, intelligence, and security.

01

Communications and Network Optimization for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks

Dynamic UAV deployment, air-ground collaborative networking, low-altitude wireless access, and network resource optimization.

Keywords: UAV deployment · air-ground networking · wireless access · network resource optimization

02

Collaborative Computing and Edge Intelligence for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks

Cloud-edge-end collaborative computing, integrated communication and computing, heterogeneous resource coordination, and intelligent scheduling.

Keywords: cloud-edge-end collaboration · communication-computing integration · heterogeneous resources · intelligent scheduling

03

Security and Trustworthy Management for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks

Secure communications and covert transmission, data and model security, malicious-attack detection, and trustworthy control and management.

Keywords: secure communication · covert transmission · data/model security · attack detection · trustworthy management

Research Scope

Across these three directions, the group studies UAV-assisted communications and computing, mobile edge computing, cloud-edge-end intelligent collaboration, network resource optimization and scheduling, federated and edge intelligence, as well as security protection for dynamic and resource-constrained low-altitude networking environments.
