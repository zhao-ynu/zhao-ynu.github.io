Research Group & Student Training

The group conducts research on collaborative computing, edge intelligence, network optimization, and security management for low-altitude intelligent networks.

Ph.D. Training

Ph.D. students mainly conduct research on collaborative computing for low-altitude intelligent networks, edge intelligence, network optimization, and security management.

Master's Training

Research-Oriented Track

Focuses on theoretical and algorithmic research in low-altitude intelligent networks, edge intelligence, and network security.

R&D-Oriented Track

Focuses on algorithm deployment, platform development, and application validation for low-altitude networks and cloud-edge-end collaborative systems.

Experimental Platform

The team has established an experimental platform for low-altitude intelligent networks and edge intelligence, providing facilities for algorithmic research, system development, and scenario-based validation.
