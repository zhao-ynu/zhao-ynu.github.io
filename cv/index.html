Curriculum Vitae

Mingxiong Zhao, Ph.D. in Engineering, is a Professor, Ph.D. Supervisor, and Vice Dean of the School of Software (School of Artificial Intelligence), Yunnan University. He is also the Head of the Yunnan Provincial Network and Information Security Supervisor Team and the Director of the Cyberspace Security Program at Yunnan University.

Academic Profile

He received the Ph.D. degree in Engineering from the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, South China University of Technology, in July 2016, and conducted visiting research at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, and the Singapore University of Technology and Design. He is a Senior Member of the China Institute of Communications, a member of its Data Security Technical Committee, a Young Editorial Board Member of the Journal of Information and Intelligence (English edition), a member of the Kunming Network Security Expert Pool, and an external reviewer for the National Natural Science Foundation of China.

He was selected for Yunnan University's “Donglu Young and Middle-Aged Backbone Faculty” Program, the Yunnan “Ten-Thousand Talents Program” as a Young Top-Notch Talent, and the first cohort of Young Scholars under Yunnan University's “Donglu Talent Program.” He was selected as a Ph.D. supervisor in 2024.

Research

His long-term research focuses on collaborative computing and security management for low-altitude intelligent networks, particularly UAV-assisted communications and computing, cloud-edge-end intelligent collaboration, network resource optimization and scheduling, and security protection.

  • Communications and Network Optimization for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks: dynamic UAV deployment, air-ground collaborative networking, low-altitude wireless access, and network resource optimization.
  • Collaborative Computing and Edge Intelligence for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks: cloud-edge-end collaborative computing, integrated communication and computing, heterogeneous resource coordination, and intelligent scheduling.
  • Security and Trustworthy Management for Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks: secure communications and covert transmission, data and model security, malicious-attack detection, and trustworthy control and management.
