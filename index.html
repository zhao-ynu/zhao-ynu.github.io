Academic Profile

Mingxiong Zhao

Mingxiong Zhao received the Ph.D. degree in Engineering from the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, South China University of Technology, in July 2016. He is currently a Professor, Ph.D. Supervisor, and Vice Dean of the School of Software (School of Artificial Intelligence), Yunnan University. He also serves as the Head of the Yunnan Provincial Network and Information Security Supervisor Team and the Director of the Cyberspace Security Program at Yunnan University.

He conducted visiting research at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, USA, and the Singapore University of Technology and Design. He is a Senior Member of the China Institute of Communications, a member of the Data Security Technical Committee of the China Institute of Communications, a Young Editorial Board Member of the Journal of Information and Intelligence (English edition), a member of the Kunming Network Security Expert Pool, and an external reviewer for the National Natural Science Foundation of China.

His research focuses on collaborative computing and security management for low-altitude intelligent networks, with particular interests in UAV-assisted communications and computing, cloud-edge-end intelligent collaboration, network resource optimization and scheduling, and security protection.

Research Profile

He has served as principal investigator for two projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, one Key Research and Development Program project in the industrial sector of Yunnan Province, and three projects under the Yunnan Provincial Applied Basic Research Program. As first or corresponding author, he has published 26 SCI-indexed papers, including 16 papers in IEEE Transactions series journals and four papers in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal. Among these publications, eight are in CAS Journal Ranking Zone 1 and thirteen are in Zone 2; six are in CCF-recommended Class A international journals and four in Class B international journals. One paper has been selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper.

His research achievements have received the Second Prize of the Yunnan Provincial Natural Science Award (ranked first), the IEEE CSE 2025 Best Paper Award, and the EURASIP JWCN Best Paper Award 2026. He has been granted 11 Chinese invention patents as the first inventor, including one patent that has been transferred for application.
