Academic Profile

赵明雄 / Mingxiong Zhao

I am a Professor, Ph.D. Supervisor, and Vice Dean at Yunnan University. My research focuses on the intersection of low-altitude intelligent networks, mobile edge computing, edge intelligence, and network/AI security.

我的研究主要围绕低空智联网中的通信、计算、智能与安全协同展开， 关注无人机辅助通信与计算、任务卸载与服务迁移、在线资源调度、 联邦学习以及智能系统安全等问题。

Research Interests

  • Low-Altitude Intelligent Networks — UAV-assisted communication, networking, and computing
  • Mobile / Edge Computing — task offloading, caching, service migration, and online scheduling
  • Edge Intelligence — federated learning, collaborative inference, and AI-RAN
  • Network & AI Security — covert/secure transmission, poisoning and backdoor defense, trustworthy aggregation
